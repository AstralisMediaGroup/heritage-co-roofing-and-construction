# Heritage Voice

Source of truth: [Build Pack §1](https://www.notion.so/34e93503be1c817aaf16da29a2806ce8) (project context + voice) and §2 (no italic emphasis).

## The voice in one paragraph

Plainspoken, local, founder-led, anti-national-chain. Sentences sound like they came out of a contractor's mouth, not a marketing deck. Short. Specific. The reader is a homeowner or a building owner in East Texas or Central Arkansas — not a "valued partner."

## Rules — do

- **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Period beats semicolon.
- **Concrete nouns.** "Roof," "phone," "shingle," "adjuster" — not "solution," "experience," "offering."
- **Direct address.** "You call. We answer." First-person plural for Heritage. Second person for the reader.
- **Specific numbers.** "30 minutes away." "15 years roofing." "Most residential roofs install in one day." Numbers beat adjectives.
- **Plain verbs.** Call, answer, climb, install, document, replace, repair. Not engage, facilitate, deliver, leverage, empower.
- **Negative space.** Stop when you've said it. If the next sentence would soften the first one, cut it.

## Rules — don't

- No marketing-deck words: **excellence, premier, industry-leading, world-class, trusted partner, comprehensive solutions, customer-centric, best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, holistic, end-to-end, value-add.**
- No italics for emphasis. (Build Pack §2 — locked.)
- No exclamation points.
- No three-comma run-ons.
- No superlatives without a number behind them. "The best roofers in East Texas" is bragging. "30+ combined years on East Texas roofs" is a fact.
- No "passionate about." Nobody is passionate about roofing. They're good at it.
- No "we pride ourselves on." The phrase itself is corporate tell.

## Voice by copy type

- **Hero H1:** 4–9 words. Statement of fact or product. "Roofs for your home." "Storm damage? We handle the whole roof." Two short clauses with a `<br>` is fine.
- **Hero subhead:** One breath. ~20–35 words. Lead with the concrete thing the reader gets.
- **Bullets:** 3–6 words. Noun phrases. "Free roof inspections" not "We provide complimentary inspection services."
- **FAQ answers:** 3–5 sentences. Lead with the answer. Then the why. Then the qualifier if needed.
- **Founder bios:** One short paragraph. Role + specific cred + one concrete detail. No "passionate," no "dedicated."
- **Trust strips:** 3 short noun phrases, dot-separated. "Insurance accepted · Workmanship warranty · Locally owned."
- **CTAs (button text):** 2–5 words, imperative or possessive. "Get my free inspection." "Request quote." "Call 903-242-8102."
- **Meta descriptions:** 140–155 chars. Sound like a sentence a person would say, not a keyword stuffing.

## Positive examples (✅ ship as-is)

- "We answer the phone."
- "When you call, you get us."
- "We're 30 minutes away if something needs attention."
- "Most residential roofs install in one day."
- "It is our name on the truck."
- "Our crews live in your area, work in your area, send their kids to school in your area."
- "Storm damage? We handle the whole roof."

## Counter-examples (❌ rewrite to ✅)

- ❌ "Customer service excellence" → ✅ "We answer the phone."
- ❌ "Industry-leading workmanship and unparalleled quality" → ✅ "Workmanship and material warranties on every job."
- ❌ "Your trusted local roofing partner" → ✅ "Our crews live in your area."
- ❌ "Comprehensive roofing solutions tailored to your needs" → ✅ "Replacement, repair, storm damage. Insurance accepted."
- ❌ "We are passionate about delivering superior results" → ✅ "It is our name on the truck."
- ❌ "Experience the Heritage difference" → ✅ "Locally owned. Locally crewed. Same people on the phone as on the ladder."
- ❌ "Seamlessly navigate the insurance claims process" → ✅ "We talk to your adjuster. You stay out of the back-and-forth."

## Self-check before output

Before you submit a paragraph, ask:

1. **Could this come from a national chain's website?** If yes, rewrite.
2. **Is there a marketing-deck word in here?** (See banned list.) Cut it.
3. **Are there italics, exclamation points, or run-ons?** Remove.
4. **Could a sentence include a specific number, place, or concrete detail and doesn't?** Add the number.

If all four answers are clean, ship it.

## What this skill does NOT cover

- Code, component scaffolding, JSX/Astro markup
- Plan documents, Notion meta-docs, commit messages
- READMEs, internal docs, status updates to Alec

For those, voice doesn't apply — write normally.
